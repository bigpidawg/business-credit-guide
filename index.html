01. The Corporate Veil

Business credit is about separating your personal life from your business identity. If you don't set this up correctly, the banks will always look at you instead of your company.

The Setup Checklist

  • LLC or Corp: Must be active and in good standing with the Secretary of State.
  • EIN: Your business's social security number.
  • D-U-N-S Number: Register with Dun & Bradstreet immediately.
  • Professional Address: No P.O. Boxes. Use a virtual office if needed.
  • Business Phone: Needs to be listed in 411 directories.
  • Business Email: name@yourbusiness.com (Avoid @gmail/yahoo).

02. Tier 1: Net-30 Vendors

You need at least 5 tradelines that report to Experian Business, Equifax Business, and D&B. These are your "starter" accounts.

Quill / Uline

Office supplies. Easy approval, reports fast. Use for regular small orders.

Grainger

Industrial supplies. Great for building volume and showing diversity.

03. Tier 2: Retail Credit

Once you have 5+ Tier 1 tradelines reporting, you move to store cards. These are higher limits and more useful for daily operations.

The Retail Powerhouses

  • Amazon Business: The holy grail for operations. Pay-in-full accounts often report to all three bureaus.
  • Lowe's / Home Depot: Essential for real estate or physical businesses. Often available without a PG once Tier 1 is established.
  • Staples / Office Depot: High approval rates for businesses with a 6-month history.

04. Tier 3: Fleet & Gas

Fleet credit is one of the easiest ways to secure $5,000 to $10,000 limits without checking your personal credit score.

WEX Fleet

Universal fuel cards. Reports to Equifax and Experian Business.

Shell / BP Business

Dedicated fuel lines. Perfect for logistics or service-based businesses.

05. Elite Funding (Tier 4)

This is where we use your 750+ Personal FICO as a "Bridge" to secure high-limit business credit cards ($20k-$50k each) that do not show up on your personal report.

The Funding Strategy

By leveraging your personal score for a "Personal Guarantee" on these elite cards, you get the limits you need while keeping your personal utilization at 0%.

  • Chase Ink Business: 0% interest for 12 months. Does not report to personal.
  • Amex Business Gold/Platinum: High-limit charge cards for major expenses.
