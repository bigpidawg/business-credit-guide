01. The Personal Audit

Before transitioning a client to Business Credit, the Agent must verify that the Personal Restoration phase is 100% complete. Transitioning too early will result in "High Risk" flags.

Minimum Requirements

  • FICO 8 Score: 680 minimum, 720+ preferred across all 3 bureaus.
  • Derogatories: Zero active collections or late payments in the last 24 months.
  • Utilization: Personal utilization must be under 9%.
  • Inquiries: Fewer than 3 hard pulls in the last 6 months.

02. Lender Compliance

The Agent must ensure the client's business identity is bulletproof. Most denials are not due to credit, but due to "Lender Compliance" failures.

Verification Checklist

  • Professional Email: Client must NOT use @gmail. Use Google Workspace or similar.
  • Website: Must have a live, professional landing page.
  • Phone Listing: Business number must be registered with ListYourself.net or 411.
  • Address: Verify it is NOT a residential address or UPS store. Use a commercial virtual office.

03. Tradeline Velocity

The Agent’s goal is to build a "Thick" business profile as fast as possible. Follow this specific sequence:

Tier 1: Foundation (Month 1-2)

Agent orders 5 Net-30 accounts (Quill, Uline, Grainger, etc.). Ensure client pays within 10 days of the invoice to maximize the Paydex score.

Tier 2: Expansion (Month 3-4)

Once 5 tradelines report, Agent applies for Fleet Cards (WEX/Fuelman) and Store Cards (Amazon/Lowe's) without using the client's SSN where possible.

04. The Funding Wave

This is the payout phase. The Agent coordinates a "Wave" of applications to secure maximum capital before the inquiries hit the report.

The 24-Hour Blitz

Apply in this exact order to leverage the most "lenient" bureaus first:

  • Chase Business Ink: Apply first (strictly 5/24 rule).
  • American Express Business: Same day (Amex often uses a single pull for multiple cards).
  • Local Credit Unions: Final step for high-limit lines of credit.
